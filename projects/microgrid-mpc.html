Microgrid Energy Management & Rolling-Horizon MPC

An uncertainty-aware microgrid optimization framework comparing day-ahead scheduling and rolling-horizon model predictive control under renewable and load forecast uncertainty.

Research Problem

Renewable-rich microgrids must operate economically while responding to uncertain generation, changing demand and grid-availability conditions. This project studies how optimization and receding-horizon control can reduce the operational impact of forecast error.

Modeling Approach

  • Hybrid PV–wind–diesel–battery microgrid model.
  • Critical and non-critical load representation.
  • Grid-connected and islanded operating scenarios.
  • Day-ahead deterministic/stochastic scheduling compared with rolling-horizon MPC.
  • Operational constraints for generation, battery energy, power balance and reliability.

Research Direction

The broader objective is to quantify uncertainty cost and evaluate how stochastic scheduling and continuous re-optimization can improve economic performance and resilience without sacrificing critical-load service.
