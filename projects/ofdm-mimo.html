Overview

I developed this project individually to explore intelligent link adaptation in a fading MIMO channel. Instead of relying on a single fixed transmission mode, the system adapts modulation and power settings according to estimated channel conditions.

Communication Chain

  • 64-point OFDM with 48 data subcarriers, pilot tones and a 16-sample cyclic prefix.
  • 2×2 spatial multiplexing over quasi-static Rayleigh fading with AWGN.
  • Pilot-based least-squares channel estimation.
  • MMSE equalization at the receiver.
  • Four modulation schemes combined with five discrete transmission-power levels, forming 20 candidate actions.

Adaptive Intelligence

Channel-quality information is used to select transmission actions that balance reliability and resource efficiency. The system provides a platform for comparing fixed transmission strategies with intelligent adaptation under changing channel conditions.
