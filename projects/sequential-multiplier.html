Overview

The project focused on the register-transfer-level design and simulation of a synchronous sequential multiplier using a finite-state-machine controller and a structured datapath.

Design & Verification

  • Designed a 3×3 unsigned parallel sequential multiplier.
  • Developed the FSM controller, datapath and ASM-chart representation.
  • Implemented the design in SystemVerilog and verified functionality in Active-HDL.
  • Used Xilinx Vivado (AMD) as part of the FPGA/digital-design workflow.
  • Validated sequential operation under a verified 10 MHz clock.

My Contribution

As team leader, I coordinated the four-member team and contributed to architecture development, RTL implementation, simulation, verification and documentation of the FSM-based multiplier.
