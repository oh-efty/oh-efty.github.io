Overview

This team project explored intelligent underwater waste retrieval by combining visual perception with robotic manipulation. The system was designed to identify underwater debris and support automated retrieval through a vision-guided feedback workflow.

System Functions

  • Detection and classification of underwater waste categories such as plastic, metal and organic objects.
  • Vision-guided feedback for aligning a robotic grabbing mechanism with a detected target.
  • Integration of sensing, robotic motion and automated retrieval logic.
  • Hardware assembly, simulation and systematic testing of the retrieval workflow.

My Contribution

As team leader, I coordinated the two-member project, contributed to the perception-and-retrieval workflow, supported hardware and software integration, and helped validate the overall robotic system.
