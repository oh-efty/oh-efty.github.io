Advanced Cybersecurity of DER-Integrated Power Systems / Microgrids

Research on cyber attacks, their operational impact and advanced detection/mitigation strategies in distributed-energy-resource-integrated grids and microgrids.

Research Scope

A DER-based power system or microgrid will be modeled with resources such as solar PV, wind generation, batteries and inverter-based resources. Selected cyber attacks will then be introduced to study their impact on cyber-physical operation and to develop suitable detection and mitigation approaches.

Cyber Attacks to Be Studied

  • False Data Injection (FDI).
  • Denial-of-Service (DoS).
  • Command injection.
  • GPS / time spoofing.

Impact Assessment

The research will evaluate how attacks affect voltage, frequency, system stability, protection behavior and energy-management performance in DER-integrated power systems and microgrids.

Detection, Classification & Mitigation

Cyber-attack detection, classification and mitigation methods will be developed or compared using system measurements and cyber-physical operating data.

Advanced Methods Under Consideration

  • AI / Deep Learning: detection and classification of abnormal or cyber-attack patterns from system measurements.
  • Quantum ML / DL: feasibility-based investigation or comparison of quantum-enhanced learning with conventional ML/DL for complex attack detection.
  • Federated Learning: distributed training of attack-detection models across DERs/controllers without centrally sharing all raw data.
  • Blockchain: stronger trust, data integrity and secure verification of measurements and commands among distributed devices.
  • Digital Twin: a virtual replica of the cyber-physical system for attack simulation, monitoring and safe testing of mitigation strategies.
  • Reinforcement Learning (optional): adaptive response or control actions after an attack is detected.

Possible Tools

MATLAB/Simulink, PSCAD, ETAP, DIgSILENT PowerFactory and Python-based AI/ML frameworks may be used depending on the final modeling and methodological choices.

Research Development

DER-integrated grid/microgrid model and cyber-physical communication/control structure.

Detection & Mitigation

Method development, comparison, validation and performance assessment.
