Research Problem

Renewable-rich microgrids must remain economical and reliable while responding to forecast uncertainty in renewable generation and demand. This research quantifies the operational impact of uncertainty and evaluates strategies that can mitigate it.

Modeling Approach

  • Hybrid PV–wind–diesel–battery microgrid model.
  • Critical and non-critical load representation.
  • Grid-connected and islanded operating scenarios.
  • Deterministic and stochastic day-ahead scheduling.
  • Rolling-horizon model predictive control with repeated re-optimization.
  • Operational constraints for generation, battery energy, power balance and reliability.

Research Contribution

The completed study quantifies uncertainty cost and evaluates how much of that cost can be mitigated through stochastic scheduling and recovered through rolling-horizon MPC. It also examines resilience under constrained and islanded operating conditions.

Current Status

The research work is complete and is intended for ICREST conference submission. The public portfolio currently presents the research scope and methodology; selected results and paper materials can be added later.
